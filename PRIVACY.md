# Privacy Policy — Curator

Curator is a private, personal fork of the Jellyfin Android TV app, built for use with
a specific self-hosted Jellyfin media server. It is not distributed publicly and is not
intended for use with any server other than the one it was built for.

## What data the app handles

- **Login credentials and session tokens** — used only to authenticate with your
  Jellyfin server. Stored locally on your device. Never sent anywhere except that
  server.
- **Crash and diagnostic reports** — if enabled (on by default, can be turned off in
  Settings), crash logs are uploaded directly to your own Jellyfin server's log
  endpoint, authenticated with your existing session. They are not sent to Google, to
  the developer, or to any third-party analytics service.
- **Viewing preferences and genre preferences** — stored on your Jellyfin server
  against your user account, the same way standard Jellyfin favourites/preferences
  work.

## What the app does not do

- No advertising, no ad tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs.
- No data is sold or shared with third parties.
- No data leaves the device except to communicate with the Jellyfin server you connect
  it to.

## Contact

This app is maintained privately for a small group of invited testers. If you have
questions, contact the developer directly.
